Account control

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your account directly in the iPhone app or on the website. You do not need to contact support.

In the iPhone app

  1. Open the Account tab.
  2. Scroll to Danger zone.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

On the website

  1. Open the Yahtzee website and sign in.
  2. Open the menu and select Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Review the warning and confirm.

What deletion removes

  • Your AWS Cognito sign-in account and email association.
  • Your private first name and surname.
  • Your public username and its reservation.
  • Leaderboard scores associated with your account.
Deletion is permanent. It cannot be undone, and the same username may become available to another person.

Information stored on your device

Saved game state and remembered Real Dice player names are local. Delete the app or clear the website’s storage to remove them.

Need help?

If you cannot access your account, email info@ijrhservices.co.uk from the account email address. We may need to verify account ownership.
